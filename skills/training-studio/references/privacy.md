# Privacy and authorisation

## What stays local

- Selected documents, scenario files and everything derived from them stay in the harness and the training directory.
- Private documents are never sent to a search engine, an external OCR service or any upload service.

## Public searches

Build each public query from established public names, concepts and versions. Before it is used, read it for customer names, internal URLs, project names and document titles. `harness-research` rejects the obvious cases; whether ordinary words disclose something private is your judgment.

## What needs the user's go-ahead

- Creating or changing a Google Slides deck.
- Uploading anything to Yoodli or another service. Training Studio prepares local files. Creating or enabling a roleplay in the service is the user's action.
- Sharing or publishing any deliverable.
- Installing software or changing account settings.

A go-ahead covers further actions of the same kind on the same destination: permission to update a deck covers the later updates to that deck. A new destination, or a wider scope, needs asking again.

## Exports

An export contains everything in its source. If any source was private, say so before the user shares the export outside their organisation.
