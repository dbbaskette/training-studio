## What drawing cannot put right

This deck was reviewed against the design the person approved. If a slide of yours can be put right only with another visual concept than the one approved, or with other words, do not change either: draw the nearest you can within the approved design, and name the slide in `beyond` when you hand in, with why in a sentence. The person decides that, not you: the runner takes what the deck says back to them with your reason.
