## This blueprint is for level {level}

The training has levels. A level is a depth tier of the same subject, and each assumes the one before.

| Level | Depth | In field enablement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 101 | What it is, why it matters, core vocabulary. No prerequisites | Positioning and the pitch |
| 201 | How it works, common tasks, standard use cases | Discovery, qualification, demonstration |
| 301 | Architecture, tuning, troubleshooting, trade-offs | Technical deep dive; objections from engineers |
| 401 | Internals, design at scale, edge cases | Architecture workshops; competitive displacement |

- Put the line `Level: {level}` in the frame, and say there what the learner is assumed to have from earlier levels.
- Do not teach an earlier level again. Name that material as assumed, and do not state an objective that belongs to an earlier level.
- Size this level on its own.
- The research was done once for every level. Use the findings that fit this depth, and leave the rest to the other levels.
